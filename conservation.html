    Conservation status

    Conservation status sets a species' spawn tier. These categories describe the fictional creatures in this game.

    Every species gets its weighted spawn score from its conservation status. Least Concern starts at 8,388,608. Each tier has half the spawn score of the previous tier. The seven Alien tiers follow Extinct and run from Alien Least Concern to Alien Extinct. Alien Extinct has a score of 1,024, leaving ten further halvings before reaching 1. An egg's chance in an area is its score divided by the total score of all species in that area. Species in the same tier have the same score. An area with only one species gives it a 100% species chance. Egg quality is rolled separately.
