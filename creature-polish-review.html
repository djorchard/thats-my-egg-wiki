Remaining acceptance

Local body repairs preserve the supplied visible surfaces, UVs and original maps. Puglet retains four forward-facing cloven hooves. The remaining problems are recovery coat deformation and performance polish, not a reason to reverse the feet or replace the creature design.

Current body renders and held candidates are offline source playback at their listed speeds. The previews omit random facial overlays.
