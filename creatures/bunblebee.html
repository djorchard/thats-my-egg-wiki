Measurements from the current creature catalogue.

Parent reference masses: Bumblebee (0.25 g) and Rabbit (2.00 kg). Parent reference sizes: Bumblebee: 0.015 m (body length); Rabbit: 0.44 m (body length). Pet weight equals average parent mass multiplied by (actual pet height in metres / average parent size) squared. One stud is exactly 0.28 metres. Low-bodied parents use body length; the other recorded heights and bird body lengths are size proxies. These are game balance references, with range midpoints and explicit estimates rather than measured population means. The 4-stud minimum and quality height bonuses count towards pet weight. Egg weight uses its separate volume rule.
A Standard pet at size 1 hatches at 4 studs (1.12 m) and 24.24 kg. The base statistics describe the catalogue dimensions before the hatch height minimum.

Currency earnings

Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency is Cash. Normal income scales with saved size and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. Followers do not earn garden income; active petting temporarily gives the petted creature 3x. See Game mechanics for the full rules. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
