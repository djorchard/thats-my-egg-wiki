Measurements from the current creature catalogue.

Moving to Farmland in the design lineup. The current gameplay catalogue still includes it among the original starter species.
Base weight is the mean of Chicken (3.40 kg) and Kitten (4.75 kg). Reference masses use reported adult means or published-range midpoints for the selected animal types. Base heights use a deliberate 4 to 5.5 stud visibility range beside a 5 to 6 stud character. A 4-stud minimum is retained after individual size rolls; quality height bonuses are applied afterwards.

Currency earnings

Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency name and quality bonuses are not assigned. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
