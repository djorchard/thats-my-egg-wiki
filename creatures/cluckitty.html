    About Cluckitty

    A plump featherball with pointed ears, bright eyes and a tiny beak. Cluckitty purrs in warm nesting places, then springs up to chase any leaf that tumbles past.

    Parent reference masses: Chicken (3.40 kg) and Kitten (4.75 kg). Parent reference sizes: Chicken: 0.7 m (standing height); Kitten: 0.225 m (shoulder height). For each parent, use the larger recorded height or length. Natural standard size blends 75% of the heavier parent dimension and 25% of the other parent dimension. Compress that natural size to its standard baseline, then apply the saved quality roll directly to dimensions and mass. Model proportions stay fixed. Creature kg uses the heavier parent mass and the actual dominant-dimension ratio. Egg kg is about 10.0892% of actual creature kg. Raw egg height scales with the same roll, followed by a 1.5-stud minimum that does not change kg. One stud is 0.28 metres. Some parent measurements remain body-length, shoulder-height or diameter proxies, and explicit game estimates remain labelled. See the current worked calculation and scale tables below.

    Current parent-size blend

    Kitten is the heavier parent. Natural target height = 0.75 x 0.624255 + 0.25 x 0.7 = 0.64319125 m, or 2.297111607143 studs.

    Models longer than they are tall match forward length; the others match height. All dimensions scale together, preserving proportions. The natural standard dominant dimension is compressed to the 3 to 14 stud baseline range before the saved quality roll is applied. Individual quality scales can exceed that baseline range. Long bodies can be less than 3 studs tall. The parent measurement types above remain explicitly labelled.

    Currency earnings

    Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency is Cash. Normal income scales with saved size and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. Followers do not earn garden income; active petting temporarily gives the petted creature 3x. See Game mechanicsXP and skill levels for the full rules. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
