About Cockapoodle

A lively curly-coated companion with soft hanging ears, a sunny crest and feathered wings. Cockapoodle bounds along on four gentle paws, lifting its feathered tail whenever something catches its attention.

Parent reference masses: Cockatoo (800.00 g) and Poodle (24.95 kg). Parent reference sizes: Cockatoo: 0.5 m (body length); Poodle: 0.61 m (shoulder height). Pet weight uses the retained species calibration multiplied by relative height and relative front-to-back length. Both dimensions scale together, so individual mass still scales quadratically. Length includes the tail and uses installed model depth normalized to standard height; it is an extent, not a curved body measurement. One stud is exactly 0.28 metres. Low-bodied parents use body length; the other recorded heights and bird body lengths are size proxies. These are game balance references, with range midpoints and explicit estimates rather than measured population means. Pet height is base height times its internal scale, with no four-stud floor or extra quality height bonus. Pet kg uses scale squared. Egg kg is the base creature kg times scale, and base egg dimensions multiply directly by scale. See the scale tables for the quality bands.
A Standard pet at internal scale 1 hatches at 3.55 studs (0.994 m) and 40.17 kg. The base statistics describe catalogue dimensions at scale 1.
