    About Geckidna

    Olive-green scales, cream-tipped quills and warm, curious eyes give Geckidna its distinctive appearance. Four sturdy legs end in broad gripping toe pads, while one thick tail steadies it as it explores beneath fallen leaves.

    Animal combination: Gecko + Echidna
