About Hedgehopper

A friendly green garden hopper with a large head, glossy black eyes and a gently tapered hedgehog muzzle. Fine dark-green spines with pale-green tips flow from its crown across the entire back and down both sides, above a smooth segmented olive-green underside. Two small front legs and two large folded jumping legs support its wingless body, while slender pointed antennae rise above its rounded green ears.
