Parent reference masses: Ruby-throated Hummingbird (4.00 g) and Big Dipper Firefly (0.05 g). Parent reference sizes: Ruby-throated Hummingbird: 0.08 m (body length); Big Dipper Firefly: 0.014 m (body length). Pet weight uses the retained species calibration multiplied by relative height and relative front-to-back length. Both dimensions scale together, so individual mass still scales quadratically. Length includes the tail and uses installed model depth normalized to standard height; it is an extent, not a curved body measurement. One stud is exactly 0.28 metres. Low-bodied parents use body length; the other recorded heights and bird body lengths are size proxies. These are game balance references, with range midpoints and explicit estimates rather than measured population means. Pet height is base height times its internal scale, with no four-stud floor or extra quality height bonus. Pet kg uses scale squared. Egg kg is the base creature kg times scale, and base egg dimensions multiply directly by scale. See the scale tables for the quality bands.
A Standard pet at internal scale 1 hatches at 2.8 studs (0.784 m) and 0.44 kg. The base statistics describe catalogue dimensions at scale 1.

Currency earnings

Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency is Cash. Normal income scales with saved size and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. Followers do not earn garden income; active petting temporarily gives the petted creature 3x. See Game mechanicsXP and skill levels for the full rules. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
