About Koalameleon

A soft-eared climber with a broad dark nose, gentle eyes and a tightly curled tail. Koalameleon's coat shifts through shades of green as its mood changes, helping it settle quietly among the leaves.

Pet weight uses the retained species calibration multiplied by relative height and relative front-to-back length. Both dimensions scale together, so individual mass still scales quadratically. These are game balance references, with range midpoints and explicit estimates rather than measured population means. Pet height is base height times its internal scale, with no four-stud floor or extra quality height bonus. Pet kg uses scale squared. Egg kg is the base creature kg times scale, and base egg dimensions multiply directly by scale. See the scale tables for the quality bands.

Currency earnings
