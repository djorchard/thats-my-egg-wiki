    About Squeakapede

    Squeakapede rustles through the garden on a rippling row of pink paws, sniffing for seeds beneath fallen leaves. Its soft grey rings bunch gently whenever it stops to wiggle its antennae.

    Animal combination: Millipede + Mouse

    Hatch bonuses

    First hatch: +2,000 Running XP and $5,000 Cash. Later hatches: +200 Running XP (10%), with no further discovery Cash.

    One first-hatch award per player and species, shared across qualities and sizes. The certificate shows both credited amounts with the skill icon. Fixed amounts are independent of XP boosts. All hatch bonuses

    Assigned game stats. The current creature model is installed alongside its existing egg and named wild spawning. Gameplay acceptance and developer review remain separate.

    Parent reference masses: Millipede (204.12 g) and Mouse (21.00 g). Parent reference sizes: Millipede: 0.3429 m (body length); Mouse: 0.08 m (body length). For each parent, use the larger recorded height or length. Natural standard size blends 75% of the heavier parent dimension and 25% of the other parent dimension. Compress that natural size to its standard baseline, then apply the saved quality roll directly to dimensions and mass. Model proportions stay fixed. Creature kg uses the heavier parent mass and the actual dominant-dimension ratio. Egg kg is about 10.0892% of actual creature kg. Raw egg height scales with the same roll, followed by a 1.5-stud minimum that does not change kg. One stud is 0.28 metres. Some parent measurements remain body-length, shoulder-height or diameter proxies, and explicit game estimates remain labelled. See the current worked calculation and scale tables below.
    A Standard pet at internal scale 1 hatches at 1.04251 studs (0.291903 m) and 0.58 kg. The base statistics describe catalogue dimensions at scale 1.

    Current parent-size blend

    Millipede is the heavier parent. Natural target length = 0.75 x 0.3429 + 0.25 x 0.08 = 0.277175 m, or 0.989910714286 studs.

    Models longer than they are tall match forward length; the others match height. All dimensions scale together, preserving proportions. The natural standard dominant dimension is compressed to the 3 to 14 stud baseline range before the saved quality roll is applied. Individual quality scales can exceed that baseline range. Long bodies can be less than 3 studs tall. The parent measurement types above remain explicitly labelled.

    Currency earnings

    Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency is Cash. Normal income scales with saved size and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. Followers do not earn garden income; active petting temporarily gives the petted creature 3x. See Game mechanicsXP and skill levels for the full rules. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
