About Pisangitang

A curved banana-shaped creature with banana-yellow skin beneath shaggy orange orangutan fur. Thick fur covers its body and back, leaving its friendly face, broad hands and feet exposed, with small glimpses of yellow skin between the fur. Long arms and short legs complete its playful silhouette.
