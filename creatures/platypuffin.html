About Platypuffin

Warm brown feathers, cream cheek patches and a broad orange bill give Platypuffin a cheerful face. It waddles along quiet shores, paddles through the reeds and takes short, busy flights over the lake. Its wide flat tail leaves little ripples wherever it goes.

Compress that natural size to its standard baseline, then apply the saved quality roll directly to dimensions and mass. Model proportions stay fixed. Current parent-size blend

Platypus is the heavier parent. Natural target length = 0.75 x 0.465 + 0.25 x 0.275 = 0.4175 m, or 1.491071428571 studs.

Models longer than they are tall match forward length; the others match height. All dimensions scale together, preserving proportions. The natural standard dominant dimension is compressed to the 3 to 14 stud baseline range before the saved quality roll is applied. Individual quality scales can exceed that baseline range. Long bodies can be less than 3 studs tall. The parent measurement types above remain explicitly labelled.

Currency earnings
