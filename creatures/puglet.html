About Puglet

A plump little pig with a pug's expressive brown eyes, floppy ears and dark muzzle. Its pink pig snout, tiny hooves and curled pig tail keep its farm identity clear.

Base weight is the mean of Pig (328.854 kg) and Pug (7.25748 kg). Reference masses use reported adult means or published-range midpoints for the selected animal types. Base heights use a deliberate 4 to 5.5 stud visibility range beside a 5 to 6 stud character. A 4-stud minimum is retained after individual size rolls; quality height bonuses are applied afterwards.
