About Tapirrot

Tapirrot trots between jungle roots, sniffing out ripe fruit with its soft snout. Its bright crest bobs as it explores, and its red, yellow and blue wings open to carry it across streams.

Parent reference masses: Tapir (240.00 kg) and Scarlet Macaw (1.09 kg). Parent reference sizes: Tapir: 2.165 m (head/body length, excludes unmeasured tail); Scarlet Macaw: 0.85 m (published head-to-tail length proxy). For each parent, use the larger recorded height or length. Natural standard size blends 75% of the heavier parent dimension and 25% of the other parent dimension. Compress that natural size to its standard baseline, then apply the saved quality roll directly to dimensions and mass. Model proportions stay fixed. Creature kg uses the heavier parent mass and the actual dominant-dimension ratio. Egg kg is about 10.0892% of actual creature kg. Raw egg height scales with the same roll, followed by a 1.5-stud minimum that does not change kg. One stud is 0.28 metres. Some parent measurements remain body-length, shoulder-height or diameter proxies, and explicit game estimates remain labelled. See the current worked calculation and scale tables below.

Current parent-size blend

Tapir is the heavier parent. Natural target length = 0.75 x 2.165 + 0.25 x 0.85 = 1.83625 m, or 6.558035714286 studs.

Models longer than they are tall match forward length; the others match height. All dimensions scale together, preserving proportions. The natural standard dominant dimension is compressed to the 3 to 14 stud baseline range before the saved quality roll is applied. Individual quality scales can exceed that baseline range. Long bodies can be less than 3 studs tall. The parent measurement types above remain explicitly labelled.

Currency earnings

Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency is Cash. Normal income scales with saved size and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. Followers do not earn garden income; active petting temporarily gives the petted creature 3x. See Game mechanicsXP and skill levels for the full rules. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
