Home guide

Gardens and upgrades

Start with 3 pets. Buy land from your garden's For sale sign and grow to 30 pets across 16 upgrades.

Your garden plot

Each player receives a Home plot. Grass covers the purchased part of an occupied garden. The remaining land is brown studded Plastic, with alternating light and dark squares on the 15-stud grid. All ground surfaces meet the grey courtyard floor.

Unoccupied plots remain entirely brown Plastic, with no grass terrain or sale sign. Your saved garden returns when you join and your profile loads. Its grass and sign are removed when you leave.

The label above your garden shows your name and pet count, for example PlayerName 1/10. Only hatched pets assigned a garden slot count. Stored pets and unhatched eggs are excluded. A following pet still uses its reserved garden slot.

How to expand

  1. Find the For sale sign on the brown land just beyond your current grass.
  2. Check the price and extra pet slots printed on its board.
  3. Approach the sign and hold its Expand interaction to purchase the land.

You must own the plot, be alive and carry no egg. Your profile must be ready and you need enough coins. Visitors cannot buy your land.

After a purchase, the grass expands and the sign moves into the next unpurchased strip. The sign disappears after upgrade 16. Eggs and creatures remain grounded on the purchased area; flying creatures keep their normal hover.

All 16 upgrades

The starting capacity is 3 pets. Upgrade 1 adds 5, upgrades 2 to 8 add 2 each, and upgrades 9 to 16 add 1 each, reaching 30 pets.

The first purchase costs 50,000 coins. Prices are ten times their previous values and increase with each purchase. The table lists each individual purchase, not the cumulative cost.
