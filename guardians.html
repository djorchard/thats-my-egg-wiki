    Guardian health and knockouts

    Club hits reduce a guardian's HP. Sunnyside Backyard starts at 70 HP, exactly three hits at Strength 1 without a damage bonus. Health increases linearly through the progression order above to 540 HP in Moonbeam Expanse. Players damage the same shared health pool.

    Reaching zero HP knocks the guardian out for 8 seconds. It wakes with full health. You can hit a guardian while it patrols or chases. Knocked-out guardians cannot be hit until they wake, and further swings do not extend their timer. Home, night safety and the developer Guardian Nap remain protected.

    Attack damage from Strength

    Base club damage rises linearly from 30 HP at Strength 1 to 90 HP at Strength 100, exactly three times the starting damage. The formula is 30 + 60 * (Strength level - 1) / 99 HP per hit, using your level when the swing starts. Heavy hitter multiplies that damage by 1.2. The same club damage calculation applies to players, parents and guardians. Examples below are rounded to two decimal places; actual damage retains its precision.

    Passing objects

    Guardians cross fences directly and can reach players on fence tops through supported hops or flying reach. They do not route around fence ends. Authored fence spans retain native pass-through navigation while physical player collision and supported landings remain. Home remains protected.

    Traps affecting guardians is planned work only.

    How guardian speed works

    Chase uses the chased player's normal unburdened speed, including training and the equipped trail, clamped to the independent biome range above. Egg weight, Strength and stamina do not change boss speed. The shared chase delay is 0 seconds. Backyard starts at 94% of target speed, the main route rises to 101% in Jungle, and each water, fire and sky branch continues at 102%, 103% and 104%. Ratios belong to the guardian's home biome, so replacing a prototype or crossing biome boundaries retains its difficulty.

    Stopping does not stop the guardian. Temporary immobilization, trap slows, Home movement limits and developer movement multipliers do not reduce or inflate its chase reference. In water, the reference uses separate unburdened swimming training; running trails do not multiply swimming.

    Patrol is calibrated by species and guardian size. The non-Creeper profiles favour a natural walking, hopping or cruising pace. Larger versions scale speed by the square root of height relative to their fitted reference. Creepers retain their existing speed. Recovery remains independently tuned at 7 studs/s (1.96 m/s). Outside its home biome, a returning guardian moves at 2 times its own latest chase speed, including when carrying an egg. A guard standing still or sleeping is not moving at patrol speed.

    Egg return inside its home biome uses the recovery baseline multiplied by 2.5. The faster outside-biome return replaces this multiplier. Guardians recover their own caught and dropped eggs across biome boundaries while preserving Home protection. A guardian carries at most one egg; if it catches a second thief while full, that thief's egg drops only when supported ground is available.

    These values are movement targets. Obstacles, turns, pauses, jumps and route choice can change actual travel time. Chase, carry and multiplayer acceptance remains To do until tested.

    Compare with player training

    Normal running speed increases at level boundaries. The calibrated running curve reaches 150 studs per second at level 100 with Lightning, or about 113 without a trail. The ordinary running ceiling also caps sprint bursts. Automatic bursts multiply player movement by 1.3 before that ceiling; recovery movement uses 0.7. Normal close pursuit retains its biome calibration. Distant catch-up and warned straight-line charges can temporarily increase guardian pace. The level-20 held player boost is excluded from the guardian reference and can exceed the ordinary player ceiling. Cash trails add +20%, +40% and another twenty percentage points per tier through +320% to the running stat benefit before diminishing returns and calibration. Trails do not multiply final movement speed or award XP. Base native Swimming speed rises from 14 studs per second at level 1 to 150 at level 100, without running trails. Guardian swimming limits retain their earlier curve.

    Strength sets comfortable egg carry capacity. Within it, carrying speed uses 1 - 2 * (1 - 1 / sqrt(1 + 0.6 * eggKg / capacityKg)). Overweight eggs remain carryable at half speed and show a warning. Stamina drain is 1 + min(eggKg / capacityKg, 2) times normal: double at capacity, rising to triple at twice capacity, then capped. Dropping an egg and increasing Strength help the player without speeding up the boss.

    The tables compare ordinary close pursuit. Distant catch-up raises chase pace, and warned committed charges create a separate dodge opportunity; ordinary speed thresholds alone do not guarantee escape. Chase movement is continuous rather than teleporting. Stalled returns have their separate guarded recovery policy. Turns, obstacles, head start, stamina and route choice remain important, with live balance acceptance pending.
