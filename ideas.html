Planned

Today's focus, 27 October 2026

Complete the first two areas to five assigned creatures with actual models, playtest existing features and fix bugs, hear music through Forest in-game, and audit existing interactions for missing sound effects before adding the confirmed gaps. This is a dated milestone, with unchecked work still pending.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, DAILY_FOCUS_2026-10-27.md.

Planned

Expanded creature pools in farther areas

Aim for seven species in regular areas and fourteen in farther areas, including the final areas. Keep existing nests and egg-slot counts, drawing from a larger species pool. Choose the progression boundary and individual species assignments before implementing the expanded rosters. The current starter milestone remains five each.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, CREATURES.md.

Planned

Rewards and achievements

Add player rewards and achievements. Goals, reward types, claiming, saved progress and repeat behavior still need design. Existing discovery and record ribbons do not establish that an achievement reward system exists.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Planned

Area completion boss battles

Give every area its own boss battle, unlocked after collecting all of that area's eggs, with a reward for winning. Define what collection completion means, how it persists, boss mechanics, rewards and repeatability. Current roaming guardians and stick knockouts do not implement these completion battles.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, COMBAT.md.

Planned

Game-themed clothing and avatar wearables

Create themed clothing and avatar wearables for players to own and wear beyond this game wherever supported. Choose designs, earning or claiming rules, distribution, publishing requirements and supported platforms before implementation.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Partly implemented

Purchasable swimming pool training

The separate saved swimming stat and water movement are implemented. Buying a Home swimming pool and practicing to increase that stat remain planned. Pool price, footprint, training rate, upgrades and owner interaction are undecided. Treadmill running points remain independent.

Current swimming and breath mechanics

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, WATER_BIOMES.md.

Needs decision

Prehistoric Ocean and the later Lost World request

An earlier backlog request names Prehistoric Ocean after Deep Ocean. The newer implemented route adds Lost World after Deepwater Abyss as a dinosaur island reached by surfacing from deep water. Lost World is the current mapped prototype; a separate additional Prehistoric Ocean is not evidenced as implemented.

The old backlog entry is retained for traceability. Confirm whether a separate ocean area is still wanted before adding another biome or treating the earlier name as an agreed rename.

Current Lost World region

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, LOST_WORLD.md, WATER_BIOMES.md.

Concept

Purchasable server boosts

Consider paid boosts shared by everyone in the current server: better rare-egg chances, higher money earnings and faster Speed training. Prices, strengths, durations, stacking, purchase handling, visibility and departure/server-close rules are undecided.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Concept

Weight training for carrying strength

Consider weights that train carrying strength so heavier eggs cause less movement slowdown. Decide the strength curve, training interaction and relationship to the existing running Speed stat. Current egg-weight carry penalties do not include this progression.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Concept

Creature levels

Consider pet levels and experience. How experience is earned, what levels change, rewards, presentation and saved progression are still undecided.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Planned

Speed sensation effects

Add effects that make movement feel faster, including a wider field of view at higher speed. Tune against actual movement and restore the normal camera smoothly when slowing, stopping or respawning. The current Speed HUD and movement curve do not complete this visual feature.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Partly implemented

Weapons and player attacks for egg theft

The stick pickup, player hit damage, forced egg drops, Home safety and guardian knockouts are implemented. Broader weapon choices, weapon progression or additional attack systems are not specified as complete. A hit releases an egg through guarded custody rules rather than promising an automatic transfer to the attacker.

Current stick combat

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, COMBAT.md.

Partly implemented

Main shop

The bottom-dock Shop window currently sells stored pets. General stock, prices, shopping, purchases and saved item ownership remain planned. Individual planned trail, garden-item and house-item shops should not be mistaken for available stock.

Implemented pet selling

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, CURRENCY.md.

Needs decision

Slow mode

Plan slow mode after defining its purpose, what movement it changes, who can enable it and how it is switched off. Check interactions with carrying and existing speed controls once its behavior is chosen.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Implemented

Show player speed

The combined top HUD already shows the saved running Speed stat with the day countdown and Cash balance. This is the progression stat, not a live studs-per-second speedometer. Live layout, respawn and rejoin acceptance remain pending.

Training and actual movement speed

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, CURRENCY.md.

Concept

Money per second leaderboard

Consider ranking players by income per second. Decide eligible income, update behavior and server-local or wider rankings. The existing Richest pet page ranks each player's best individual pet; it is not a total player income leaderboard.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, RECORDS.md.

Concept

Optional ad for money

Consider optional rewarded ads for Cash. Decide whether to offer them, reward size and availability. Any implementation must award once for a completed ad and handle cancellation or unavailable ads without claiming a reward.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Planned

Trail shop

Add a trail shop after selecting trails and prices. Browsing, purchase ownership, saving, equipping, switching, unequipping and visibility to other players remain work to implement and verify.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Implemented

Sell pets

Stored-pet selling is implemented with a server quote, confirmation and one profile transaction for removal and full payment. Garden pets must first be stored and unhatched eggs cannot sell. Live UI, multiplayer and real-storage acceptance remain pending.

Selling rules and price calculation

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, CURRENCY.md.

Concept

Fuse machine

Develop the pet fusion concept before implementation. Eligible pets, number of inputs, cost, results, and quality or size effects are undecided. An approved flow would clearly show consumed pets and outputs, then preserve ownership and saved results without duplicate requests.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Implemented

Treadmill gameplay

Owner-only running training and twenty paid upgrades are implemented. Starting machines currently appear automatically for ready owners. Purchasing the initial treadmill through Shop is a separate planned step; live gameplay and persistence acceptance remain pending.

Current treadmill rates and prices

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, HOME.md, CURRENCY.md.

Partly implemented

Paid house tiers and house item shop

Basic private houses, automatic doors, bed respawns and bed sleep are implemented. Paid house tiers and purchasable furniture or decorations remain planned. Choose tier differences, currency, prices, catalogue and placement controls while preserving house privacy and the existing plot layout.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, HOME.md.

Planned

Garden item shop

Let players buy garden items. Stock, prices, ownership, placement and saved arrangements are undecided. Keep movement and room for pets and eggs. Existing land-expansion purchases do not implement a garden decoration shop.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, HOME.md.

Concept

Pet feeding

Consider whether feeding belongs in the game. Food costs, interaction, effects and any relationship to growth are undecided. No hunger, feeding penalty or feeding-dependent growth is promised.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md.

Concept

Pet growth over two days

Consider newborn pets starting at 50% of their own final size and reaching 100% after two days. Decide real-world days or play time, offline progress and any feeding relationship before implementation. Current incubating egg growth is separate from this proposed post-hatch pet growth.

Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, HATCHING.md.

Planned

Water mounts and advanced flying mounts

Route designs reserve water-mount travel and advanced flying-mount access for later progression. Mounting, travel, dismounting, access rules and saved ownership remain planned. Current native swimming and cosmetic followers are implemented separately; neither is a rideable mount.

Project sources: FUTURE_BIOMES.md, WATER_BIOMES.md, src/biome_config.luau.

Scrapped

Dedicated return lanes

Dedicated return lanes and their stripes were explicitly scrapped. Retained map builders no longer generate them, and the inspected Sky Foothills markers were removed. Future scenery has no dedicated return-lane requirement.

Project sources: FUTURE_BIOMES.md.
