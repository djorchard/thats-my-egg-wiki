    Implemented

    Hidden golden egg

    Requested as a TODO on 31 October 2026 and implemented on 1 November as the Golden Egg capture contest. One shared daily 1-in-20 chance selects one of 28 movable locations. One carrier must defend its fixed circle for twenty seconds to win a weighted $1,000,000 to $20,000,000 Cash reward. Source and static Edit previews are verified; live input, multiplayer, effects, audio and real save/rejoin acceptance remain pending. The owner can also request fresh daytime events after collection through Admin Abuse; only one Golden Egg can be active at a time.

    Current Golden Egg rules and static previews

    Project sources: DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md, GOLDEN_EGG.md.

    Implemented

    Tune guardian chase speeds

    Guardians use the chased player's normal unburdened land or Swimming speed times the biome pacing ratio, clamped to that biome's minimum and maximum. Running training and an equipped trail affect the land reference; egg weight, Strength, stamina, traps, immobilization, Home caps and developer overrides do not. A heavy egg slows only its carrier. Patrol, egg return, catching and safety retain their own rules. The Guardian page derives every current ratio and range from source. Live pursuit, carrying, multiplayer and device acceptance remain pending.
