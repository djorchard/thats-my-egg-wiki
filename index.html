    Egg quality

    Quality is rolled independently of species and conservation status. It does not change egg size or weight.

    QualityChanceAura colourColour codeEffects
    Standard65%NoneNot applicableOriginal egg appearance, no aura
    Fine25%Green#5BDE77Wider soft glow and larger sparkles
    Superior8%Blue#4BA4FFWider soft glow and larger sparkles, pulsing light, 2 orbit ribbons
    Epic1.8%Purple#B56BFFWider soft glow and larger sparkles, pulsing light, 3 orbit ribbons
    Legendary0.2%Gold#FFCD4DWider soft glow and larger sparkles, pulsing light, 5 orbit ribbons, star glints

    Aura previews

    Actual Studio captures of the same Labraduck shell at every quality. Higher qualities add stronger glow, more sparkles and orbit ribbons. These still images show one moment of each animated effect. Select an image to open it full size.
