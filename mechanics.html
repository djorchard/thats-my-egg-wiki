Implemented

Joining, welcome and the first-visit intro

After loading, the welcome screen appears once per join. It shows a random existing creature dream and the latest development update. Read full update expands the news. Let me in or X dismisses the screen. Movement and world prompts resume when the welcome closes or hands over to the first-visit intro.

The welcome announces that all player progress will reset on 1 January 2027, when official early access begins. This is the announced date, not evidence that the reset has already run.

A genuinely new profile then sees a private intro. A runner escapes a Labraduck chase from Clover Farm into the player's Home garden, leaves a Legendary egg and exits. The egg grows and opens to reveal the player's real character, then the camera settles behind them. Use Space or Skip intro to move directly to that handoff. The button also supports touch and controller activation.

The first successful profile acquisition counts as the first visit. Existing profiles, including empty profiles and reset progress, are returning players. Disconnecting before the scene ends does not schedule it again on rejoin. If the intro cannot prepare its route or assets, it releases the presentation and lets entry continue. The cinematic supplies no pet, currency or inventory reward. Source and installed Edit checks support this flow. Live first-join, skip and rejoin acceptance remain pending.

Rescue eggs and escape to Home

Take a wild egg, escape its guardian and cross into Home to secure it as your own inventory egg. Carry one wild egg at a time. Home protects players from guardians and stick attacks.

Dropping an egg ends its theft chase. Other players can collect supported public drops; a guardian recovers eligible eggs within its own biome. Delivery and ownership transfers are checked by the server so the same egg cannot be awarded twice. Inventory capacity or unavailable saving can reject a transfer without consuming its original custody.

Hold Pick up until the egg progress fills. It starts dark blue, fades through lighter blue at halfway and reaches light green at completion. Releasing early cancels the pickup and resets the progress. The server still checks that the egg is available and can be collected.

Biome lengths, stamina and travel time

Biome travel lengths follow the player route: main and fire areas use Z, while water and sky branches use X. The table uses current biome bounds. Existing widths, elevations and flying gaps remain unchanged.

Normal running uses the current trained speed, a 1.3x automatic burst and 0.7x recovery movement. Starting stamina is eight units, plus 0.25 for each further Running level, reaching 32.75 at level 100. With no carried egg, drain is one third of a unit per second, giving 24 seconds at level one. Carrying an egg drains one unit per second plus its effective weight slowdown percentage: a 20 percent slowdown drains 1.2. Strength reduces that weight penalty. Normal running starts at 16 studs per second, reaching about 113 at level 100 without a trail. Lightning reaches the ordinary 150-stud movement ceiling.

Starting or restarting an ordinary burst requires at least 50 percent stamina. Once started, it continues below that threshold until empty. Ordinary empty-to-full recovery now takes about 8.20 seconds; reaching half takes about 4.10 seconds. Standing still in Home doubles recovery, giving about 4.10 seconds to full. Entering Home preserves the current reserve, with no ordinary movement drain inside Home. Holding Shift no longer selects a walk mode or freezes stamina.

The passive stamina meter appears at the top centre once stamina drops strictly below 90 percent, then stays visible until it returns to 100 percent, with a running icon, STAMINA caption, whole percentage and twenty white segments in a rounded dark panel. The entire meter uses 80 percent opacity. Equipped trails are hidden and cleared while stamina recovers, then resume when running resumes. The trail speed benefit remains equipped. Running stamina is session-only and separate from Swimming, saved XP, training equipment, traps and developer movement controls.

Each timing below starts that biome with a full bar and includes its ordinary recovery intervals and half-bar bursts. There is no carried egg, trail, pet bonus or held boost, and movement is straight and level without stops. Consecutive journeys share the current reserve, so these separate times must not be added as a continuous-route prediction.

Water and sky rows are running-distance equivalents for comparison, not measured swimming, climbing or flying times. Water uses separate Swimming progression without running stamina. Cave ascent, beach access, turns, fences, obstacles, mounts, catches and network or device performance can change real travel time. Live traversal and balance acceptance remain pending.

The six route fence runs at Home/Backyard, Backyard/Farm and Farm/Lake are another ten percent taller: visible wood is 7.722 studs high and the collision top is 7.882. Gateways and footprints remain unchanged. Guardian hops retain eligibility; live player clearance remains untested.

Fall damage

The first 20 studs of a fall are safe. When you land, you lose 1 health point for every stud beyond 20. A 30-stud fall deals 10 damage, a 50-stud fall deals 30, and a 120-stud fall deals 100. Fractional distances deal proportional damage.

Fall distance is the vertical drop from the highest point you reach before landing. Water landings deal no fall damage and clear the fall, including shallow water before the Swimming state updates. Climbing, sitting and server teleports also reset it. Normal spawn protection still applies.

Damage is applied once on landing. Your existing health regeneration starts again one second after damage, at 1 health point per second. Installed source and offline checks are complete; live gameplay acceptance remains pending.

Five-minute day and night cycle

Each cycle has 288 seconds of day and 12 seconds of night, aligned to five-minute wall-clock boundaries. Sunnyside Backyard requests six fresh wild eggs at world initialization and each dawn, regardless of player count. Every other biome requests the connected player count plus two. Actual allocation stops at compatible authored nest spaces; it never creates overflow homes or places land-only eggs underwater. Joining or leaving during the day does not reroll the allocation. Delivered wild eggs remain depleted until dawn; owned public drops are separate.

Night clears unbanked carried custody before returning players outside Home to safety. Players already safe stay in place. Guardians sleep and a visible semi-transparent barrier closes the Backyard entrance. Garden incubation runs at 12x during night and 1x during day.

Golden Egg: claim the circle

At normal daytime startup and each dawn, the server samples its connected player count for one Golden Egg chance: player count divided by 120, capped at six players. Six players retain the original 5 percent chance; three players get 2.5 percent, and one player gets about 0.833 percent (1 in 120). An empty server cannot spawn one naturally, and later joins do not reroll the day. A successful roll chooses one location uniformly from 28 possibilities, two in each of the fourteen biomes. The automatic roll can create at most one Golden Egg per server per game day. The owner can request additional daytime events after collection, with at most one available or carried Golden Egg at a time.

The owner-only Admin Abuse section in Developer tools has Spawn Golden Egg. It bypasses only the daily occurrence die, keeps the same random location, reward and capture rules, and never replaces an active egg. A completed event can be followed by a fresh manual event without resetting the automatic daily roll. Manual events keep the normal announcement and late-join lifecycle.

The solid-gold egg sparkles and glows. A natural arrival uses the usual egg popup, native chat and one Legendary spawn sound. A successful manual spawn uses that same popup, chat and single sound cue, with Admin Abuse wording, the actual triggering administrator name and the biome. Names are displayed as text. Failed or repeated requests do not create another announcement. Late joiners are told only about a Golden Egg that is still available that day.

Use Claim once to become the carrier. Your name and a twenty-second countdown appear near your head. Stay inside the fixed gold circle until the countdown finishes; there is no button to keep holding. Only one player can control it at a time.

To take control from another player, force them outside the circle. The circle has a 15.6-stud radius, 31.2 studs across, and stays at the spawn location. It is now 30 percent wider. A knockdown inside does not break their capture. Leaving the circle, dying or leaving the server returns the egg to its centre and resets the full twenty seconds for the next claimant.

The reward has an 80% chance to be a whole-dollar amount from $1,000,000 to $10,000,000, inclusive, and a 20% chance from $10,000,001 to $20,000,000, inclusive. Egg size grows linearly from 1x at $1,000,000 to 3x at $20,000,000, so bigger eggs contain more Cash.

A successful capture bursts into coins with the usual Cash pickup popup and spatial sound. One winner receives the exact full reward directly in their Cash balance. After payment, native server chat announces the winner's name and exact dollar amount. Nearby players within 200 studs also see the green +$amount popup rise from above the winner's head. Repeated receipts do not repeat the announcement or popup. The egg does not become an inventory item or pet. Claim and the winner's coin visuals respond immediately while the server confirms ownership and payment in the background.

Unclaimed Golden Eggs disappear at night. They do not return after a five-minute loot respawn; the next dawn makes the next chance roll. The original Golden Egg model, authoring locations and static Edit previews were installed and verified. The expanded Admin Abuse announcement and timer-aware layout changes are now installed with guarded exact source readback. Native desktop, phone and Indonesian landscape fixtures verify the actor text and countdown layout, but the Golden Egg ViewportFrame art did not render inside the static SurfaceGui fixture. Popup art, playback and full-family wiki references remain pending. Offline and static Edit checks do not establish live input, competition, effects, audio or real save/rejoin acceptance. Billboard UI and animated sparkles are not visible in the retained static model previews.

Admin Abuse owner controls

Developer tools has an Admin Abuse section for the configured game owner. Ordinary developers and Admin-only accounts cannot use these event controls. The server checks the real caller; changing a client presentation flag cannot grant access.

The owner can independently set Cash and XP earnings factors from 1x to 1000x for everyone in the current server. New servers start at 1x and the factors are session-owned. Only confirmed actual changes announce the administrator. These controls are installed and source verified; game publication and live multiplayer or late-join acceptance remain pending.

Legendary egg spawn multiplier accepts whole values from 1x to 1000x and has Reset to 1x. It affects the current server only and starts at 1x in each new server. Future wild egg quality rolls, including overflow and Egg Rush eggs, use the selected value. Existing eggs, species odds and the normal dawn egg allocation stay unchanged.

Only the Legendary quality weight is multiplied before all quality weights are normalized. The displayed chance is 0.020% at 1x and about 16.669% at 1000x with the current catalogue. The multiplier does not guarantee a Legendary egg. Developer inventory grants and their selected quality are separate.

Spawn Golden Egg requires daytime and preserves any available or carried Golden Egg. It skips the daily chance die, chooses one normal authored location and rolls the usual weighted Cash reward and matching size. It starts the same twenty-second capture contest. After collection, another manual event can be requested without reopening the automatic daily chance.

Egg Rush lasts up to 60 seconds and adds two bonus wild eggs to each of the fourteen biomes, 28 total, during daytime. These eggs use the ordinary species, quality and custody rules. Unclaimed extras disappear when the event expires or normal night cleanup removes them. Legitimately carried or delivered eggs keep their normal lifecycle. The rush does not replace the normal dawn allocation.

Guardian Nap makes every existing guardian use its Sleeping behavior for 30 seconds. Chase, catch, retaliation and stick knockouts pause while the nap is active. Existing egg custody is preserved, and guardians resume normal behavior at the deadline, including when the event spans night and dawn.

Spawn Giant Egg requires daytime and chooses one compatible authored wild-egg location uniformly across the biomes. It creates one Legendary egg at the existing maximum legal 3x creature scale. This is an ordinary wild egg with its normal heavy carrying, rescue to Home, inventory, hatching and pet rules, separate from the Golden Egg Cash contest. Another Giant Egg cannot start while the event egg remains in active custody.

Successful owner actions announce the actual triggering administrator to the current server. Multiplier changes, Egg Rush, Guardian Nap and Giant Egg use the existing administrator notice. Manual Golden Eggs use their existing rare-egg popup and native chat with Admin Abuse wording, the administrator name and biome, plus one Legendary sound cue. Natural Golden Egg wording is unchanged. Failed actions, unchanged settings and repeated request receipts do not repeat a success announcement.

All players see passive Egg Rush and Guardian Nap countdowns below the main HUD and active server boosts. They use the shared server deadlines, including for late joiners, disappear on expiry or hidden HUD, and reserve space for rare-egg notices, stamina and breath. There is no Coin Shower: Daniel cancelled it, and it has no control, supported action or pending implementation idea.

Multiplier choices project immediately while the server confirms them in the background. Each event button shows its requested state immediately; shared eggs and event deadlines follow the server result. Stale replies cannot replace newer choices, browsing or filtering never starts an event, and an uncertain request is not automatically replayed.

The expanded owner controls, three events, public countdowns and actor-aware announcements are installed in Studio Edit mode with guarded exact source readback, including the whole-second timer formatting correction. Focused offline checks pass. Native static fixtures pass 33 checks for the three new control layouts; desktop, phone and Indonesian landscape fixtures also verify actor text and both countdown layouts. The Golden Egg ViewportFrame art did not render inside the static SurfaceGui fixture, so popup art and playback acceptance remain pending. Species, conservation, quality and egg size

Species and quality are independent server rolls. Conservation sets species spawn weight. Quality selects the scale band, then a bounded Gaussian roll supplies one CreatureScale shared by the egg and creature. Most rolls are near the band midpoint; extremes are rare.

Standard scales range from 0.75 to 1.25, Fine from 1 to 1.5, Superior from 1.5 to 2, Epic from 2 to 2.5 and Legendary from 2.5 to 3. The saved roll multiplies the compressed creature baseline directly. Egg kg is about 10.0892 percent of the actual creature kg. Raw egg dimensions scale with the same roll, followed by a 1.5-stud height minimum that does not change kg. Heavier eggs use the Strength capacity rules.

Superior, Epic and Legendary wild eggs use a top-centre popup with their egg and biome icons. Popup, native chat and sound share one five-second queue. Late joiners receive still-available eggs until night. Live animation, join/dawn delivery and listening remain pending.

Place eggs, incubate and hatch

Place a rescued inventory egg in your own purchased garden area. Placement is permanent for the egg: it stays there while incubating and grows visually in ten-second steps. Incubation continues offline; becoming ready does not automatically hatch it. Use Hatch when it is ready.

Base hatch work by conservation tier is 2 minutes, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours and 24 hours. Quality multiplies that work by 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5 or 3 from Standard through Legendary. The night multiplier accelerates completed work; egg size does not change the required duration.

A successful hatch opens the shell and gives the owner a saved Birth Certificate with the pet's identity, height, mass, quality and conservation status. First species discoveries receive NEW; later qualifying weight records can receive RECORD. Missing creature models use an explicit placeholder.

For a species you have never hatched, its Birth Certificate always appears before either garden-selection screen. The confirmed hatchling waits safely in Stored pets while you view its certificate and first-hatch bonus, then choose which pet to keep in the Garden. Closing the choice keeps the new pet in Stored.

Pet storage, species limits and Pick best

Pets contains Garden, Stored and Discover. Discover opens the permanent species Collection, and is selected when Pets opens. Stored pets do not use Garden capacity, and incubating eggs have their own limit. The current limits are 500 stored pets, 250 inventory eggs and 50 Garden eggs, with at most two placed pets of one species.

When a third pet of the same species hatches, the replacement dialog can store the weaker existing pet, leave the ready egg unchanged or Pick best. Pick best compares normal size- and quality-adjusted income, keeps the two highest earners and stores the other pet. Existing pets win ties. Displaced pets keep their identity and certificate; full storage rejects a transition that needs another storage space.

When the Garden is full, a two-card confirmation compares the new pet with the lowest eligible Garden earner. Select the pet to keep in the Garden; the other goes to Stored. The higher earner is recommended, with ties favoring the existing pet. The cards keep their positions, and for an already-known species the close control cancels without hatching. A first-ever species instead hatches into Stored first and shows its certificate before this choice; closing keeps the new pet safely stored. Storage space and the species cap are checked again before a confirmed hatch.

Clicking an egg selects it. Only its explicit Hatch action hatches a ready placed egg. Stored eggs must first be placed and incubated. Garden and Stored retain search and sorting, including Name from A to Z. Inventory NEW badges last for the current visit; restored pets start read after rejoining.

Garden, Stored and Merge show the selected individual's quality, income, height, weight and DNA using the shared creature detail card. Wide layouts place it beside the grid; narrow layouts stack it with native scrolling. Collection articles describe the species instead of an owned individual. Merge keeps the last inspected pet visible while you choose matching inputs, and its action shows the selected count.

Water-only pets require your garden's separate pond unlock. Until it is owned, those hatchlings and transfers remain safely in Stored. The pond adds no pet capacity.

Buy more garden land

A starting garden holds four pets. Sixteen land upgrades add one pet slot each, reaching twenty. Physical lawn growth retains the existing 45 by 60 to 90 by 150 stud progression. Buy the next strip from its For sale sign as the owner, with a ready profile, enough Cash and no carried egg.

The first land upgrade costs 100,000 Cash and raises capacity to five. The final upgrade costs 78,424,000,000 Cash. The Gardens and upgrades page derives every intermediate price and capacity from the current configuration. The sign shows the price and extra slot, moves after a purchase and disappears at the maximum tier. Saved land and capacity return when your profile loads. Pond ownership is a separate purchase.

Garden pond and water-only pets

Buy your garden's pond separately from its own For Sale sign for 2,000,000 Cash. Pond ownership is saved independently of land expansion and adds no pet slots. Its basin grows with your purchased lawn, from 32 to 64 studs across. Water depth rises from nine to fifteen studs across the sixteen land upgrades. Sloped banks let land pets enter and leave.

Water-only species have Water enabled and neither Land nor Sky. They need your own unlocked garden pond before they can be placed in the Garden, including through best-pet assignment. A water-only hatch without that unlock stays safely in Stored. Earlier water-only Garden pets without a pond move into Stored with their records preserved. Land-capable pets remain eligible before purchase.

Eggs cannot be placed inside the basin. Hatch eggs blocking the proposed basin before buying the pond or enlarging the lawn. Terrain appears after server confirmation, and repeating a completed pond purchase does not charge again. Returning restores the saved pond at the size for your current garden tier. Cash, pet earnings and petting bonuses

Cash starts at zero. Eligible hatched pets placed in the Home Garden earn while their owner is online. The active following pet also earns its normal online income, even if selected from Stored, and is counted only once if it reserves a Garden slot. Other Stored pets and incubating eggs do not earn online income. The follower income popup appears once every thirty seconds, while credits accrue normally. Eligible Garden pets earn ten percent of their normal rate while offline, up to twenty-four hours per absence. House-based wallet limits are removed; the independent global numeric safety ceiling remains.

Normal income is the species base rate multiplied by CreatureScale and quality: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. A Legendary Labraduck at scale 3 earns 480 Cash per second before temporary bonuses. Quality also shifts its scale band; the old additive height bonus is removed.

The pet currently being petted earns 3x its normal rate for its owner during the approved interaction. Other pets keep their normal rates. Animal income statistics use whole Cash per hour with grouped digits; underlying accrual remains per second. Actual credits and sale quotes remain ordinary Cash amounts.

The welcome-back popup uses the actual credited Cash award. Pressing OK emits one coin and one confetti for a positive award below 5,000, two of each at 5,000, three at 10,000 and five at 20,000. Larger milestones are ten at 50,000, fifteen at 100,000, twenty-five coins and twenty-two confetti at 250,000, and the maximum forty coins and twenty-two confetti at 500,000 or more. Low graphics halves the counts, rounded up. Opening stays quiet and OK only dismisses the already-saved reward.

Floating gold income popups appear above nearby eligible Garden pets belonging to any player, including your own. They use that pet owner's bonuses. Following pets use the same popup only once every thirty seconds; Garden pets retain their normal three-second phase. Hidden UI, unavailable owners and distant pets suppress the cosmetic effect. Actual Cash accrual remains independent.

Sell stored pets in Pets

In Pets, open Stored, select a hatched pet, use Sell and confirm its named pet, quality and Cash quote. Selling permanently removes it and it cannot be bought back. Shop no longer has a Sell tab. Garden pets must first be stored, and unhatched eggs cannot be sold.

The payout is 3,600 times normal size- and quality-adjusted income per second, rounded to whole Cash. Temporary petting bonuses do not raise it. This remains a Cash amount even though income labels use Cash per hour. Full payment and removal commit together; stale quotes, missing pets or insufficient wallet room reject the sale without removing the pet.

The sale price is one hour of that pet's normal income: income per second multiplied by 3,600, rounded to whole Cash. Saved size and quality count; temporary praise, server boosts and developer earnings multipliers do not. A pet earning $10 per second sells for $36,000.

Running levels and automatic training

Running, Swimming and Strength have separate saved XP and levels. Walking and running earn Running XP from server-measured ground distance; Swimming trains independently. Teleports, vehicles, conveyors and automatic station motion do not count as active steps. Every two valid studs add one step, and each valid ground stud adds 0.5 base Running XP; valid swimming travel adds 5 base Swimming XP per stud. Strength currently sets absolute jump height to 4 + 0.08 times its level: 4.08 studs at level one, eight at level fifty and twelve at level one hundred. Developer overrides and recovery guards remain separate.

Running starts at 16 studs per second and reaches about 113 without a trail, or exactly 150 with Lightning, at level 100. Ordinary sprint bursts are capped at 150 too. Base native Swimming travel starts at 14 studs per second and reaches 150 at level 100, gaining 136/99 studs per second per level. Its control speed is calibrated for native swimming physics; running trails never affect swimming. The next level costs 1,000 + 250 times (current level - 1) XP. Pet height, length and mass

One stud is 0.28 metres. For each parent, use the larger recorded height or length. Blend 75 percent of the heavier parent's dimension with 25 percent of the other parent's dimension, then convert to studs. The creature model keeps its proportions.

Compress that natural dominant dimension before applying quality: below four studs use 3 + sqrt(d / 4); from four to eight keep d; above eight use 8 + 6 times (1 - exp(-(d - 8) / 6)). The resulting standard baseline lies in the 3 to 14 stud range. The saved quality size roll then multiplies dimensions and mass directly, so an individual can exceed that baseline range. Long-bodied creatures can be less than three studs tall.

Sleeping, petting, hatch-reveal, missing-model and water-only displays do not forage on land. A dropped item can be taken by a player, eaten by a pet or collected as rubbish, with only one successful claim. Pet meals do not grant the player a separate food buff. Direct feeding keeps its existing refresh behavior.

Source, controlled input and isolated Edit checks cover deliberate activation, pet reach and eating presentation. Live multiplayer, mobile input and real saved transactions remain untested here.
