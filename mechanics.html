Game design guide

Game mechanics

How egg rescues, hatching, pets, training and adventures fit together. These features are recorded in the current prototype; live acceptance is still pending where the owning records say so.

Updated 27 October 2026. Implementation, artwork approval and successful live testing are separate statuses. See also Ideas and future plans.

Implemented

Rescue eggs and escape to Home

Take a wild egg, escape its guardian and cross into Home to secure it as your own inventory egg. Carry one wild egg at a time. Home protects players from guardians and stick attacks.

Dropping an egg ends its theft chase. Other players can collect supported public drops; a guardian recovers eligible eggs within its own biome. Delivery and ownership transfers are checked by the server so the same egg cannot be awarded twice. Inventory capacity or unavailable saving can reject a transfer without consuming its original custody.

Project sources: BACKYARD.md, PLAYER_DATA.md.

Implemented

Five-minute day and night cycle

Each cycle has 288 seconds of day and 12 seconds of night, aligned to five-minute wall-clock boundaries. Delivered nest eggs stay depleted during the day; dawn rolls and restores the nest eggs.

Night clears unbanked carried custody before returning players outside Home to safety. Players already safe stay in place. Guardians sleep and a visible semi-transparent barrier closes the Backyard entrance. Garden incubation runs at 12x during night and 1x during day.

Project sources: BACKYARD.md, PLAYER_DATA.md, src/backyard_config.luau.

Implemented

Species, conservation, quality and egg size

Species, quality and size are separate server rolls. Conservation status determines species spawn weight, while quality uses its own Standard, Fine, Superior, Epic and Legendary chances. A planned regional creature assignment does not automatically put that creature in a current spawn pool.

Egg size varies from 80% to 120% of the species baseline. Egg mass scales with volume and heavier carried eggs reduce movement speed. Higher qualities have their own sparkles and shell finishes. Pet mass uses a different rule.

Place eggs, incubate and hatch

Place a rescued inventory egg in your own purchased garden area. Placement is permanent for the egg: it stays there while incubating and grows visually in ten-second steps. Incubation continues offline; becoming ready does not automatically hatch it. Use Hatch when it is ready.

Base hatch work by conservation tier is 2 minutes, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours and 24 hours. Quality multiplies that work by 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5 or 3 from Standard through Legendary. The night multiplier accelerates completed work; egg size does not change the required duration.

A successful hatch opens the shell and gives the owner a saved Birth Certificate with the pet's identity, height, mass, quality and conservation status. First species discoveries receive NEW; later qualifying weight records can receive RECORD. Missing creature models use an explicit placeholder.

Pet storage, species limits and Pick best

Pets has In garden and Stored categories. Stored pets do not use garden capacity, and incubating eggs have their own limit. Current limits are 500 stored pets, 250 inventory eggs and 50 garden eggs, with at most two placed pets of any one species.

When a third pet of the same species hatches, the replacement dialog can store the weaker existing pet, leave the ready egg unchanged or Pick best. Pick best compares normal size- and quality-adjusted income, keeps the two highest earners and stores the other pet. Existing pets win ties. Displaced pets keep their identity and certificate; full storage rejects a transition that needs another storage space.

Pets and Eggs support sorting, selection and their own main windows. G toggles Pets and H toggles Eggs. Ready placed eggs have a Hatch button; stored eggs cannot bypass placement and incubation.

Project sources: HATCHING.md, PLAYER_DATA.md, src/player_profile_schema.luau.

Implemented

Buy more garden land

A starting garden holds five pets. Buy the next strip from the For sale sign on the unpurchased brown land. Sixteen upgrades grow capacity to thirty pets and expand the lawn from 45 by 60 to 90 by 150 studs. Only the owner can buy land, with a ready profile, sufficient Cash and no carried egg.

The first expansion costs 50,000 Cash. Its sign shows the current price and added pet slots, moves after each purchase and disappears at the maximum tier. Vacant plots stay brown with no sale sign. Your saved lawn and capacity return when your profile loads.

Cash, pet earnings and petting bonuses

Cash starts at zero. Hatched pets placed in the Home garden earn each second while their owner is online and eligible. Stored pets, incubating eggs and adventure followers do not earn garden income. There is no offline income.

Normal income is the species base rate multiplied by saved size and quality. Quality doubles at each tier: Standard 1x, Fine 2x, Superior 4x, Epic 8x and Legendary 16x. A 120% Legendary Labraduck earns 192 Cash per second before temporary bonuses.

The pet currently being petted earns 3x its normal rate for its owner during the approved interaction. Other pets keep their normal rates. Floating coin gains show actual awards and the top HUD shows the wallet, day countdown and saved running Speed stat.

Project sources: CURRENCY.md, HOME.md.

Implemented

Sell stored pets in Shop

Open Shop, choose one of your stored pets, review its server-quoted price and confirm Sell pet. Garden pets must first be stored; unhatched eggs cannot be sold. Selling permanently removes that pet.

The price is 1,000 times normal size- and quality-adjusted income per second, rounded to a whole Cash. Temporary petting bonuses do not raise the sale price. Full payment and removal happen together; stale quotes, missing pets and a full wallet reject the sale. Selling pets is implemented, while a general item shop remains planned.

Project sources: CURRENCY.md, PLAYER_DATA.md.

Implemented

Treadmill training and upgrades

A profile-ready Home owner currently receives a starting treadmill automatically. Train on your own belt to gain ten saved Speed points per second. Leaving the belt stops training; offline time gives no training. Visitors pass through other owners' stations and cannot use or upgrade them.

Twenty paid upgrades add ten points per second each, reaching level 21 and 210 points per second. The first costs 1,000,000 Cash and each later price doubles. The final upgrade costs 524,288,000,000 Cash.

Normal running movement follows 16 + 8 * ln(1 + SpeedPoints / 100), before carried-egg penalties and developer modifiers. The saved Speed stat is different from actual movement speed. Boarding, capture and the short supported dismount are part of the treadmill interaction. Buying the initial machine through Shop remains planned.

Project sources: HOME.md, CURRENCY.md, src/speed_progression.luau.

Implemented

Private houses, automatic doors and beds

Each assigned player has a basic Plastic stud house with glass windows, automatic front and back doors and a bed respawn point. Only the owner can enter, including when a door is open. Houses appear for occupied, ready profiles and follow base ownership.

The owner can use Sleep at the bed to lie down with rising Zs. Wake up, move or jump to get out. Bed sleep has no health bonus or saved sleep state. Paid house tiers, furniture purchases and decoration controls remain future work.

Project sources: HOME.md.

Implemented

Choose an adventure follower

Use Follow me on one owned hatched pet in either storage or the garden. Stop following returns it to its original state. A garden follower reserves its garden slot and saved position; a stored follower stays stored. Neither earns garden income while following.

The non-colliding pet keeps up with running, swimming and airborne travel using its saved size and existing animations. Selection survives character respawn during the session and clears when leaving the server. Selling it, moving it between garden and storage, resetting progress or losing profile access also ends following. Following does not provide a mount or a combat ability.

Project sources: HOME.md, PLAYER_DATA.md.

Implemented

Petting, garden wandering and naps

Garden pets wander within the purchased lawn, independently of one another. Owners and visitors can pet them with free hands. The interaction aligns player and creature, shows hearts and ends when the player moves, jumps or the context changes.

Each awake pet has a one-in-twelve nap chance per awake minute. A nap lasts five minutes, with closed eyes and rising Zs; flying pets settle to the ground. Successful petting wakes it. Naps do not stop normal income, change saved measurements or create a growth system. Petting's temporary income bonus benefits the pet's owner.

Project sources: HOME.md, HATCHING.md.

Implemented

Swimming, oxygen and drowning

Native water supports normal swimming, deliberate diving and surfacing. Neutral depth control reduces automatic upward drift during underwater egg collection. Swimming uses its own saved SwimSpeedPoints and the diminishing-return movement curve; treadmill running points do not increase water speed.

Submerged heads consume a thirty-second breath reserve. Surfacing refills it in three seconds. After breath runs out, remaining submerged deals four HP per second and lethal drowning uses guarded carried-egg drops. Running out of breath alone does not immediately drop an egg.

Low oxygen progressively warns with the meter and screen effects; confirmed drowning adds muffled breathing and bubbles. Swimming progress exists, but the purchasable Home pool and its training awards are planned.

The water route continues through Rainbow Coral Reef and native underwater Deepwater Abyss, then surfaces onto Lost World's stepped beach and dry dinosaur island. Actual swimming feel, audio and multiplayer acceptance remain pending.

Stick combat, guardian knockouts and retaliation

Pick up the shared stick under the entrance-side left Backyard tree. Each living player can take one for the session and must pick up another after respawning. A valid player hit deals twenty HP, drops a held egg through its ownership rules, knocks the victim backwards and dazes them. Victims receive a four-second protection window shared across attackers. Home protects both attacker and target.

The server checks the equipped issued tool, reach, facing, walls, life state and protection. A swing chooses one eligible player, dummy or guardian. A valid guardian hit knocks it onto its side for three seconds with closed eyes and stars, preserving any egg it already carries. The guardian is protected while down and for its first second awake.

A struck guardian retaliates against the attacker after waking, including when the attacker has no egg. Actual catch damage, death, respawn, leaving, night or replacement by another pursuing guardian can end that grudge. A player has at most one pursuing guardian across the world: a new chase replaces the previous one.

Guardian collision is conditional during runtime: egg carriers can pass through guardians, while noncarriers retain solid collision. Within an eligible theft chase, a second nearer thief does not divert the guardian. Each guardian carries at most one egg. These are prototype behaviors with live combat, physics and multiplayer acceptance pending.

Training dummy and shared coin drops

The grounded training dummy beside the stick tree drops a coin when hit with an eligible stick swing, if the server's pool has room. Coins visibly arc to nearby supported ground; there can be at most ten across the server, including coins still flying.

Any eligible nearby player can collect a landed coin for a server-chosen integer from 1 to 100 Cash. A coin pays once, and unavailable profiles or full wallets leave it collectible. Coins have no automatic expiry or night reset. The earlier hanging dummy was undone; the current dummy stands on the ground.

Project sources: COMBAT.md, CURRENCY.md.

Implemented

Discoveries, weight records and the Home board

Saved species discovery and heaviest-species history support NEW and RECORD birth presentation. Equal comparable pet masses do not count as a new record. Historical mass-only records from before the new pet-weight rule are retained separately.

The Home entrance board ranks players currently in this server on six rotating pages: Cash, trained movement speed, distinct owned pet species, richest pet, heaviest pet and tallest pet. Stored pets count in the applicable pet rankings; temporary petting bonuses do not. Page arrows change only your own display. The leading player appears on the winner podium. A separate income-per-second player leaderboard is still a concept.

Project sources: RECORDS.md, HATCHING.md, PLAYER_DATA.md, PET_WEIGHTS.md.

Implemented

Emotes and random dances

C opens the emote menu, with the existing V alias and a touch layout. Social emotes include presentation such as Sit meditation, AFK, Point and Come here. Menu choices, Stop and numbered shortcuts use the game's native interface.

Dance randomly chooses from thirty retained Club Noccachino catalogue dances. Selecting Dance again chooses a different dance from the previous one. It loops until interrupted and requires an R15 avatar. Movement, jumping, tools, carrying an egg, training, knockdown and character changes stop the active dance. Dance adds no music.

Project sources: SHARED_SYSTEMS.md, DANCE_EMOTE.md.

Implemented

Ambient, approach, chase and underwater audio

Ambient songs have quiet gaps of five to ten minutes. Joining makes one one-in-five roll for an eligible immediate ambient start; otherwise the initial quiet gap applies. Songs avoid an immediate repeat when possible.

Approaching an egg can trigger proximity suspense after its dwell, unless ambient music already has priority. An actual chase interrupts ambient playback and uses the assigned biome track or retained fallback. Approach pitch and tempo vary together, and finishing an escape has its own slowdown behavior.

Underwater processing muffles music independently of the drowning effects. Stick swings, emotes, countdowns and collectible coins have their own cues. Assigned or silently fetched audio is not proof of successful live listening; music and sound-effect acceptance remain pending.

Project sources: MUSIC.md, WATER_BIOMES.md.

Implemented

Pet height and mass

One stud is exactly 0.28 metres. Pet mass equals the arithmetic mean of its two parent reference masses multiplied by (actual pet height in metres / mean parent reference size in metres) squared. Low-bodied parents use body length; reference dimensions and estimates are recorded explicitly.

Actual pet height includes individual size, the four-stud visible minimum and quality height bonuses. These affect pet mass through the squared size ratio. Egg mass remains independent and scales cubically with egg size. Grim Peeper retains its explicit fictional mass.

Schema 19 preserves saved heights and recalculates known pet masses once. Earlier mass-only species history is retained separately rather than guessed into a new comparable record. Size and quality income multipliers do not apply pet mass as an additional earnings multiplier.

Project sources: PET_WEIGHTS.md, research/creature-stat-baselines.json.

Partly implemented

Home, biomes and progression routes

The prototype has Home and fourteen biomes. Travel starts through Sunnyside Backyard, Clover Farm, Ripplewater Lake, Acorn Woods and Jungle Wilds. Jungle owns the water, fire and sky split. The map and region pages show the current route themes.

Sky Foothills has a walkable tunnel climb toward Cloudtop Cliffs. Farther sky travel and water mounts remain planned movement progression. Later regions retain prototype eggs and guardians where full creature rosters are not implemented. Lost World's dinosaurs are static scenery, alongside its existing prototype eggs and guardian.

The eventual target is seven creature species per regular area and fourteen in farther areas, using the same nests and egg-slot counts. The exact expanded-roster boundary is undecided; the immediate starter-area milestone is five species each.

Implemented

Saved progress, controls and player feedback

Gameplay profiles retain owned eggs and pets, births, garden placement and expansion, Cash, running and swimming progress, treadmill level, discoveries and species records. Incubation uses saved timestamps and progresses offline; income and training do not. Follower selection and temporary boosts remain session-owned.

Profiles use server-owned session locks, migrations and separate Studio storage. Unreadable data is not reset to an empty profile. Pending real-storage, rejoin and multiplayer checks remain separate from source verification. Six Home plots are the design target; place admission settings require their own verification.

Main windows switch exclusively. On mobile, covering full-screen windows temporarily hide the bottom action rows, restoring them after the last covering window closes. Gameplay errors use the shared error banner. Developer testing tools have permission checks and are not ordinary player progression.

The Developer Panel's To review list contains implemented checks awaiting playtester results. Bugged collects the actual failure explanation. Feedback uses thumbs up or down plus one text box, separate from bugs and review completion. Planned game ideas belong on the future-plans page.
