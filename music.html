    Ambient music and biomes

    Music selection rules and the current song catalogue.

    How selection works

    When a new ambient song starts, your current biome is sampled. If it has assigned songs, there is a 50% chance to choose only from those songs. The other 50% uses the full ambient catalogue, which also includes assigned songs. With no assignments, the normal full catalogue is used.

    Crossing into another biome does not restart or reselect the current song. Its biome-based tempo stays fixed for that playback. Chase music, shop music and boombox selections keep their own rules. Quiet gaps and music settings still apply.

    Consecutive repeats are avoided when the chosen pool has alternatives. A biome with just one assigned song may repeat it. The final chance of hearing an assigned song can exceed 50%, because it is also eligible in the full pool.
