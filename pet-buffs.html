Following pet buffs

Installed feature. Gameplay acceptance remains pending.

One active creature total, following or mounted. Choosing another replaces the current creature. Non-mountable species grant one fixed following buff. Mountable species can be ridden. Normal area limits, server validation and saved progression remain in force. No new paid boost or quality scaling.

🔋 Stamina Saver

20% less stamina used by running and gym training. Action costs are unchanged.

🌬️ Second wind

Stamina recovers 20% faster.

👟 Fleet foot

10% faster running, within area speed limits.

🌊 Swift Swimmer

15% faster swimming.

💪 Strength Boost

While this pet follows you, it boosts your Strength by 20% (up to level 100). Carry heavier eggs, jump higher and use less stamina for club swings.

🥊 Heavy hitter

20% more club and direct water-bomb damage. Trap damage and knockout duration are unchanged.

❤️ Health Boost

Double maximum health. Changing pets preserves your health percentage.

🪙 Pet Payday

15% more online income from garden and following pets. Does not increase other Cash rewards or offline income.

🦘 Spring step

20% higher jumps. Jumping uses no stamina.

🍯 Honey proof

Immune to sticky honey traps while this pet follows you.

🪶 Soft landing

50% less fall damage. The safe fall distance stays the same.

💚 Fast Healing

Natural health regeneration is 50% faster, after the usual damage delay.

🫧 Deep lungs

50% more underwater breath. Changing pets preserves the remaining percentage.

🐟 Gills

Breathe underwater without using breath or taking drowning damage while this pet follows you or is mounted.

📚 Quick learner

10% more movement, action and equipment training XP. Fixed hatch rewards are unchanged.

trap_honey Trap reach

Place traps 25 studs farther away while this pet follows you.
