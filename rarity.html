Egg guide

Egg rarity

Egg rarity describes its quality tier. See conservation status for species spawn tiers and creatures grouped by category.

Quality also selects the internal scale band. Compare creature scale, egg weight and carry-speed effects.

Egg rarity (quality)

Quality is rolled independently of species and conservation status. Its size band contributes to the shared creature scale, which also sets egg dimensions and weight.
