Rarity and conservation status

Egg rarity describes its quality tier. Conservation status belongs to the creature species. These are separate traits.

Egg rarity (quality)

Quality is rolled independently of species and conservation status. It does not change egg size or weight.

Conservation status

Conservation status is the proper name for the species categories shown on creature profiles. These labels describe fictional creatures in this game.

Categories below run from Least Concern to Extinct. Creature comparisons use this same order, followed by species whose status has not been assigned. A conservation status does not determine an egg quality roll.
