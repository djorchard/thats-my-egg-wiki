Sunnyside Backyard

Garden companions, pond explorers and tiny creatures hiding among the flowers.

Ordered from Least Concern to most endangered, with unassigned creatures last.

Daniel tells Codex when he approves how a model looks and works. Codex records Daniel's approval and updates this shared site. Reviewed shows a tick when Daniel is happy with the in-game model and animations.

Base rates are per second for a creature placed at Home. Eggs still incubating and creatures in inventory do not earn. Currency starts at $0. Hatched pets placed in your Home garden pay into your wallet every second while you are online. The currency name and quality bonuses are not assigned. Grim Peeper pets earn $10 per second.
