Farmland

Farm animals with a surprising second side. Each hybrid starts with a familiar farm animal.

Ordered from Least Concern to most endangered, with unassigned creatures last.

Daniel tells Codex when he approves how a model looks and works. Codex records that approval and its own completed review, then updates this shared site. Reviewed shows a tick only after both approvals.

Creatures are planned to earn currency passively each second while placed at Home. The currency name and individual base rates are to be decided.
