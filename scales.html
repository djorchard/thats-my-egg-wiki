    Size and carrying guide

    Creature scale and egg weight

    One internal scale controls each creature and its egg. See the size, weight, income and movement effects below.

    Our size and weight rules

    One stud is 0.28 metres. For each parent independently, use its larger recorded height or length. Choose the heavier parent by adult weight.

    1. Natural creature size: 75% of the heavier parent dimension plus 25% of the other parent dimension. Divide metres by 0.28 for studs. Keep the model proportions.
    2. Small and large creatures: compress the natural standard baseline first. Below 4 studs use 3 + sqrt(d / 4); from 4 to 8 keep d; above 8 use 8 + 6 x(1-exp(-(d-8)/6)). The baseline longest dimension approaches 3 studs for tiny creatures and 14 for huge ones. Then multiply height, length, creature kg and egg kg directly by the quality size roll. Legendary uses 2.5 to 3 times baseline, typically 2.75. The final individual can exceed 14 studs.
    3. Creature weight: heavier parent kg x actual creature dominant dimension in metres / parent dominant dimension in metres.
    4. Egg height: 1.8 x sqrt(standard creature height / 4) x (standard creature kg / 4)^(1/6), then multiply by the saved size roll and apply a minimum height of 1.5 studs. The minimum changes dimensions only.
    5. Egg weight: actual creature kg x 0.100892154843, about 10.0892%.

    The egg fraction is the median of 4,840 usable bird species, chosen to resist outliers. Three impossible egg-heavier-than-adult records were excluded. Source dataset.

    Some parent references remain labelled body-length, shoulder-height or diameter proxies. Four dog outlines are game estimates. The detailed tables identify the inputs and sources.

    A bell-shaped size roll

    New scales use a bounded Gaussian (normal, or bell-shaped) distribution. Most rolls are near the quality band's midpoint. The standard deviation is one sixth of the band width; samples beyond the limits are clamped, so exact minimum and maximum rolls are rare but possible. The roll is server-owned and stays with that creature.

    What scale changes

    The natural standard dimensions are compressed first, then the saved quality size roll multiplies height, length and mass directly. Height and length keep the same proportions. Creature mass uses the resulting quality-scaled dominant dimension; egg mass is the median fraction of that actual mass. Egg height multiplies its raw blended height by the saved roll, then applies a 1.5-stud minimum without changing kg. Normal income retains its existing roll and quality multipliers.

    How egg weight affects carrying

    Comfortable carry capacity is 1024.3774239400427 * (Strength / 90)^1.183202626193978 kg. Let load be egg kg divided by capacity. Within capacity, speed reduction is 2 * (1 - 1 / sqrt(1 + 0.6 * load)). Overweight eggs remain carryable at half speed and show a warning.

    Stamina drains at 1 + min(load, 2) times the normal unburdened rate. At capacity it drains twice as fast; at twice capacity or above it drains three times as fast. Strength increases capacity, while Running determines the stamina reserve. Existing Home, swimming, mount and training rules remain.

    Carry speed ranges below use Strength 1 and show the percentage of normal movement before other modifiers. Egg weights and dimensions show the full quality band; most rolls are near its midpoint.

    Saved scale and older creatures

    Schema 27 saves CreatureScale, species, quality and identity/history. Known creature measurements, egg measurements and normal income are computed from the catalogue and scale. Runtime UI/model projections may hold computed fields; the profile writer removes duplicate measurement fields from durable records. Species weight records persist their scale and compute kg when read.

    Older size rolls map deterministically into their quality band without rerolling. IDs, ownership, quality, hatch dates, positions and incubation deadlines remain. Sizes, weights and future earnings adopt the new formulas; previously earned Cash is retained. Historical archived records and unknown legacy species remain preserved. New rolls, rather than migration, use the bell-shaped distribution.

    Sources: CreatureCatalog, CreatureScale, EggTraits, EggBirthRules, PetIncomeRules, BackyardConfig and profile schema 27. Live carrying, models, UI, multiplayer and real storage acceptance remain pending until executed.
