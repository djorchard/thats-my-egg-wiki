    XP and skill levels

    Train Running, Swimming and Strength independently, from level 1 to 100.

    Current implemented balance. Gameplay feel, public-pool swimming, equipment input and real save/rejoin acceptance remain pending.

    What earns XP?

    Run on the ground for 0.5 Running XP per valid travelled stud. Swim for 5 Swimming XP per valid travelled stud, including deliberate ascent and descent. Delivering an ordinary wild egg grants 20 Running XP. Swimming movement and treadmill XP use their current increased rates; other movement and delivery awards retain their separate tuning. Saved XP and level thresholds are unchanged. Two travelled studs count as one step; XP boosts never multiply steps.

    Moving while carrying an egg also earns Strength XP. Each travelled stud grants 0.5 times min(4, 0.5 + ln(1 + egg kg) / 4) Strength XP. Heavier eggs train Strength faster. Teleports, vehicles, conveyors, airborne travel, controlled movement and equipment travel cannot manufacture movement XP.

    The free public pool at the back-left of Home contains native water. Swim around it to earn ordinary Swimming XP. No pool purchase or training prompt is needed for public swimming.

    How the levels scale

    From level L, the next level costs 1,000 + 250 times (L - 1) XP. Costs are ten times the previous tuning. Level 1 to 2 costs 1,000 XP; level 2 to 3 costs 1,250; level 3 to 4 costs 1,500. At the same XP rate, each successive level takes longer.

    Reaching level 100 needs 1,311,750 cumulative XP per skill. The level cap is 100; XP is saved independently for each skill. Existing earned cumulative XP is retained, and levels are recalculated using the new thresholds. This can lower a previously displayed level without erasing earned XP.

    Running starts at 16 studs per second and gains about 0.980 per level before trails, reaching 113.0 at level 100 without a trail. Equipped trails alter the Running benefit, with an earned running ceiling of 150. Swimming starts at 14 actual studs per second. Swimming gains +1.37374 per level and reaches 150.0; running trails do not affect it. Swimming values account for native Humanoid swim speed. Player Stats and the optional HUD speed readout show the corresponding actual swim speed. Egg carrying, pet buffs, traps and Home limits can change those values.

    Running unlocks

    • Level 10: Sneak. Hold Ctrl to sneak, or tap Ctrl to toggle. Uses stamina.
    • Level 20: Sprint. Hold Sprint for extra speed.
    • Level 40: Second Wind. Stamina recovers 25% faster.
    • Level 60: Efficient Stride. Sprinting uses 25% less stamina.
    • Level 100: Tireless. Running and sprinting use no stamina.
    • While hidden, movement uses 30% of your normal speed. Close discovery is 35 studs, even through cover. From Forest onward, clear sight detects normal movement at 60 studs or sneaking at 30 studs.

    Stats shows whether each milestone is locked or unlocked. Hold Ctrl on land to Sneak until release, or tap Ctrl to toggle Sneak on/off. A tap lasts at most 0.25 seconds. Controller L2 and the shared touch action remain hold controls. Sneak needs grass or cover from the guardian and more than 35 studs of distance. A pursuing guardian shows ?? and keeps heading to your last seen position at half its normal chase speed. Nearby searching uses that same pace. It looks around for two seconds only after reaching that point, then searches nearby. Within 35 studs it spots you, shows !!! for one second and resumes chasing. Release a held action, tap Ctrl again after toggling, or leave cover to stop hiding. Ctrl retains its swimming action underwater.

    Sneaking drains 0.5 stamina units per second before pet drain modifiers, even while standing still or in Home. A new hold needs at least 25% stamina. At zero, hiding stops; recover and press again to restart. Jumping, club attacks, traps, Sneak and training equipment still spend stamina at level 100. Action reserves recover gradually when not hiding, including while moving. Swimming and mounts keep their separate rules. These unlocks follow your saved Running level.

    What Strength changes

    Strength sets comfortable egg carry capacity: 1024.3774239400427 * (level / 90)^1.183202626193978 kg. Within capacity, speed reduction is 2 * (1 - 1 / sqrt(1 + 0.6 * eggKg / capacityKg)). Above capacity, eggs remain carryable at half speed and show a weight warning. Running stamina drain is 1 + min(eggKg / capacityKg, 2) times normal: double at capacity, triple at twice capacity, and capped at triple. Strength does not change Running stamina capacity or recovery.

    Strength makes stick swings faster and cheaper. At Strength 1, a swing costs 1.25 stamina units and takes about 0.556 seconds. At Strength 100, it costs 0.25 units and runs at 2x speed, taking about 0.278 seconds. The retained animation and the repeat-input lock use the same speed. Each swing must finish before another can start, and insufficient stamina blocks the swing.

    For Strength level L, progress = (L - 1) / 99. Swing speed = 1 + progress; stamina cost = 1.25 times (1 - 0.8 times progress). Cost falls linearly to 20% of its starting value. Running level increases the available stamina pool, rather than multiplying these fixed costs. Each placed trap or thrown Water bomb costs 2 units at every Strength level. Failed trap uses and repeated network requests do not spend stamina or stock. Swings use the character's client-facing direction at activation, without mouse targeting or automatic turning.

    The Strength details show maximum comfortable carry capacity in kg. Increasing Strength makes the same egg a smaller fraction of capacity, reducing both its speed penalty and stamina drain. Overweight eggs remain carryable; they do not block pickup.

    Strength sets an absolute jump height of 4 + 0.08 times the level: 4.08 studs at level 1 and 12 studs at level 100. The maximum displayed bonus is +8 studs. This is jump rise, not a promise that every obstacle of that height can be cleared. Grounded jump starts spend one stamina unit, including in Home.

    Automatic running and recovery

    Running level 1 starts with 8 stamina units, increasing by 0.25 each level to 32.75. Automatic running uses 1.3x the ordinary baseline and exhausted recovery movement uses 0.7x. A new burst starts at half the bar. Ordinary empty-to-full recovery takes 8.20 seconds; restarting at half takes 4.10. Home entry preserves the reserve and ordinary movement has no stamina drain, and standing still there recovers 2x as fast.

    The former Shift walking mode is removed. At Running level 20, hold either Shift key, controller L3 or the Boost action for a temporary speed increase. A held boost can use the entire remaining stamina reserve at 6x the current egg-aware drain, adding 30% speed in an ordinary burst or 15% during recovery. Recovery pauses during a boost. Release after depletion before another hold can start. Home, traps, mounts and jetpack restrictions remain. This escape burst is excluded from guardian reference speed. Proposed extra boost visuals remain pending specific performance approval.

    Equipment and XP multipliers

    Buy a treadmill for 210,886 Cash or a weights bench for 3,071,947 Cash in the shop. Both appear inside your house and follow house upgrades. Use Train while inside the bench station to start or stop automatic Strength training. Leaving, death, carrying an egg, losing the station or another movement controller stops training. Friends may borrow idle treadmills and weights benches, with immediate owner priority. Only the trainee earns XP, using the station tier and their own XP benefits. Ownership and upgrades stay with the owner. Borrowing rules and limits use ordinary saved progress.

    The treadmill earns 1 Running XP per second at equipment level 1, increasing by 0.25 per level to 6 at level 21. A full stamina bar lasts 5 minutes at Running 1 and 60 minutes at Running 100, independently of equipment tier. The weights bench earns 0.5 Strength XP per second, increasing by 0.075 per equipment level to 2 at level 21. Upgrade owned equipment in Stats. Train Swimming by moving through water, including the free public Home pool. Active exploration and carrying beat ordinary equipment rates. No offline training is awarded.

    Permanent XP tiers are 2x, 4x and 8x. Only the highest owned tier applies; they do not stack. Their products are configured for server-validated native Roblox checkout. The native prompt shows the offered price; a completed prompt alone does not establish a granted purchase. Temporary server treadmill boosts retain their separate existing rules. Trails change movement benefits, never earned XP.

    Developer controls

    Developers can set their own Running, Swimming or Strength to a whole level from 1 to 100. The server sets that skill's XP to its exact level boundary and schedules normal saving. It does not grant steps or alter another player. Direct player-speed controls are removed. Developer access is required on every request.

    Player skill records

    The Home records board includes Fastest runner, Quickest swimmer and Strongest player. Running and Swimming rank their independently trained base speeds in studs per second. Strength ranks skill level. These are ready players in your current server, with six displayed rows and deterministic UserId ties. Trails, carrying, temporary movement effects and developer multipliers do not inflate the skill records. Each viewer can change pages independently.

    Creature hatch XP bonuses

    The first genuine hatch of each species awards its full assigned skill bonus. Every later hatch of that species awards 10%, including another quality or size. First-hatch history survives selling, storing and rejoining. Previously discovered species use the repeat reward immediately; no retroactive award is created.

    These are fixed XP amounts, independent of permanent and server XP boosts. They are added in the same profile change as the hatch and appear on the birth certificate with the skill icon. The first hatch also awards Cash according to the species endangerment tier, shown on the same certificate. Later hatches give 10% XP without another discovery Cash award. Failed or replayed hatches, pet merges and developer-granted eggs or pets do not award hatch bonuses. Retired assignments never reactivate spawning.
