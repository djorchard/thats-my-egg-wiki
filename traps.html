Traps

Catch rivals, protect your escape and make a splash.

Five deployed traps total. Three of any one type. Water bombs in flight and their puddles use the same slots. Home is a safe area.

Bubble trap

40,000 per use

Floats a rival upward until they jump 3 times to pop it.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 seconds.

Sticky honey

10,000 per use

Slows a rival for four seconds.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 seconds.

Spring pad

50,000 per use

Launches a rival straight up 30 studs. Steering resumes on landing.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 seconds.

Banana peel

5,000 per use

10 HP damage. Trips a rival and flings their carried egg forward.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 seconds.

Water bomb

25,000 per use

15 HP damage. Knocks rivals over and drops their egg. Misses leave a slippery puddle for 10 seconds.

Up to 3 of this type. Puddle lifetime: 10 seconds.

Fake Egg

75,000 per use

Copies the nearest wild egg. Collecting it starts a 3-second beeping fuse. Drop it with F and escape the blast, which knocks you back and stuns for 4 seconds. Look for the crooked seam.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 seconds.

Buy, equip and use

Buy all six trap types in the Shopopotamus Traps aisle or through the Shopkeeper. Each wooden crate opens the category with E. Unused stock saves up to 9,999 per type.

Equip and activate to place a trap. X, middle mouse or controller B cancels. There are five deployed traps total and three per type. Ground traps arm after 1.25 seconds and expire after three minutes. Aiming too far clamps the ground preview into range. Water bombs use a red cursor beyond their Strength-based throwing range.

Bubbles float upward until three local escape jumps pop them. Honey slows for four seconds. Spring pads launch 30 studs. Your own traps can catch you. Fake eggs beep for three seconds after pickup; drop with F before their four-second stun blast. Home stays safe.

Fake egg fuse

A reflective rainbow shell marks the aiming preview. On placement, the fake copies the nearest wild egg at 125% size, with a crooked seam as its tell. Pickup starts three seconds of beeps. Press F, controller Y or Drop egg, then move away. Its 10-stud blast knocks players back and stuns them for four seconds with orbiting stars. It grants no real egg and deals no HP damage.

Water bomb landing

Throw range is 15 + Strength level / 5 studs. The cursor is red beyond range or without valid ground. Choose one landing point; the bomb follows a cosmetic arc and resolves its splash there. Nearby guardians are provoked. Puddles last 10 seconds. Your own traps can catch you; Home stays safe.

Stock and placement

Unused stock saves up to 9,999 per type. Ground-trap previews clamp into range when aiming too far. Honey gains two extra studs and Tarantulama adds 25 studs (7 metres) to ground placement. X, middle mouse or controller B cancels. Shop crates open Traps with E.
