Traps

Catch rivals, protect your escape and make a splash.

Five deployed traps total. Three of any one type. Water bombs in flight and their puddles use the same slots. Home is a safe area.

Bubble trap

40,000 Cash per use

Traps a rival for 6 seconds. Jump 3 times to escape.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 sec.

Sticky honey

10,000 Cash per use

Slows a rival for four seconds.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 sec.

50,000 Cash per use

Launches a rival straight up 30 studs. Steering resumes on landing.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 sec.

5,000 Cash per use

10 HP damage. Trips a rival and flings their carried egg forward.

Up to 3 of this type. Untriggered lifetime: 180 sec.

Water bomb

25,000 Cash per use

15 HP damage. Knocks rivals over and drops their egg. Misses leave a slippery puddle for 10 seconds.

Up to 3 of this type. Puddle lifetime: 10 sec.

Buy, equip and use

Buy a Banana peel for 5,000 Cash, Sticky honey for 10,000, a Water bomb for 25,000, a Bubble trap for 40,000, or a Spring pad for 50,000 in the Shop Buy tab. Buy 1, 5 or 10 uses at the displayed total. Unused stock is saved and appears in the hotbar after login or respawn.

Equip a ground trap and left-click to place it. Equip a Water bomb, aim and activate to throw instead. Right-click cancels; touch uses equipped-tool activation; controller R2 uses and B cancels. Each player can have five deployed traps total and at most three of one type, counting airborne water bombs and puddles. Ground traps arm after 1.25 seconds and expire after three minutes if untriggered. Ground traps cannot be placed beneath another player. Water bombs use a target cursor instead of a placement preview, with a centered target on touch and gamepad. Each successful placement or Water bomb throw spends 2 stamina units, independent of Running and Strength levels. Insufficient stamina blocks use. Stock and stamina are committed together; failed placement, missing stock and replayed requests spend neither.

Bubbles hold a rival for up to six seconds while retaining their carried egg; jump three times to pop one early. Honey slows movement for four seconds. Spring pads bounce rivals about 10 studs high. Banana peels briefly knock rivals over and fling their carried egg forward. A direct Water bomb hit knocks a rival over and drops their carried egg; a miss leaves a large slippery puddle for 10 seconds. Your own traps cannot catch you. Home stays safe. Honey, Bubble traps and Spring pads deal no damage. Banana peels deal 10 HP damage, and Water bombs or puddles deal 15 HP damage. Water splashes on impact and the splash effect fades quickly.

Water bomb: hit or miss

Equip a water bomb to use the target cursor, then aim at a rival and activate to throw. The bomb flies through the air and splashes on impact. A direct hit knocks them over and drops their carried egg through the normal egg-drop rules. It uses no direct damage.

A miss landing on safe, supported ground leaves a 28-stud-wide water puddle. Rivals can slip on it until it disappears 10 seconds after landing. The puddle remains after a slip; each victim gets the usual five-second trap immunity. Puddle slips use the banana-style fall.

Your own bombs and puddles cannot trip you. Throws from Home and puddles inside Home are blocked. A direct hit frees the deployment slot; a miss keeps it occupied until the puddle expires.

Stock, safety and controls

Buy 1, Buy 5 or Buy 10 at the displayed total. The selected trap stays selected for repeat buying. Unused stock saves, up to 99 per type. Used traps are not refunded.

Left-click or touch activation uses the equipped tool. Controller R2 uses it. Right-click or controller B cancels. Ground traps show a placement preview and cannot be placed beneath another player. Water bombs use a target cursor, or a centered target on touch and gamepad. The label shows the type count and five-trap total; a full limit turns it red. Different players have separate limits.

Deployment, hits, egg drops and purchases are checked by the server. Live multiplayer, physics, mobile controls and real saved-stock acceptance still need playtesting.
