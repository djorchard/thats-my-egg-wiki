    Unused animal ideas

    70 available animals, dinosaurs and mythical creatures for future combinations.

    How this list works

    These are design ingredients, not new game creatures. They have no approved pairing, biome, statistics or model. Scrapped designs do not count as used. Retained concept drafts are listed separately.

    Prefer recognisable, appealing animals. Exclude flies, maggots, mosquitoes and similar pest-style choices from this shortlist.

    Available means this named source type is absent from current non-scrapped pairings and retained concept drafts. Scrapped designs may be reused. Related animal families can already be represented; those cases are labelled.

    Dinosaurs are future dinosaur-island sources. Mythical sources are reserved for later areas. These entries are ideas, not approved assignments.

    Reference images show real animals or published depictions, not proposed game designs. Select an image to see its source and full credit. Countries beneath an animal identify its national or cultural associations.

    Requested animals already represented

    • Isopod: Manapill. Its Common Pillbug source is an isopod.
    • Deer: Meerkabou and Goosemoose. Caribou and moose are deer.
    • Fox: Fennthorn. Its source is Fennec Fox.
    • Chipmunk: Badgermunk. Its source is Eastern Chipmunk.
    • Tyrannosaurus rex: Albatrossaurus Rex.
    • Triceratops: Tricerahops.
    • Dragon: Dragorilla and Ferretwyrm.
    • Phoenix: Bearbecue.
    • Unicorn: Scorpicorn.

    Lemur, toucan, camel and swan were already on the shortlist. Snail is available again because Snailhog is scrapped.
